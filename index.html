What this is about

🔒

Privacy & Digital Rights

We believe everyone deserves control over their digital life. We advocate for strong encryption, data minimization, and resistance to surveillance.

🌐

Open Source & Open Tech

We support and build free and open source software. Knowledge should be shared, not locked behind paywalls.

👥

Community & Local Action

Houston needs its own voice in the fight for digital freedom. We meet regularly to learn, build, and organize.
