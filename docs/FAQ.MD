## What is LibreHTX 2026?

LibreHTX 2026 is a Houston meetup focused on free software, open source, digital rights, privacy, and practical collaboration.

## Do I need technical experience?

No. Beginners are welcome.

## Is this only for FSF supporters?

No. People from free software, open source, privacy, policy, and general technology backgrounds are all welcome.

## Why are people using different terms?

Because the communities overlap but emphasize different things. Free software emphasizes user freedom. Open source emphasizes practical collaboration. Libre often bridges the two.

## Is there a glossary?

Yes. See the [Lexicon](docs/lexicon.md).

## Why is this meetup recurring?

Because recurring events help build trust, community memory, and momentum.

## What is the relationship to the EFF?

Many people see a strong intersection between software freedom and digital rights, especially around privacy, user control, and resistance to unnecessary surveillance.
