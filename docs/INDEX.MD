## What this is about

- Learning the difference between free software, open source, and libre.
- Making the conversation approachable for new people.
- Bringing together local technologists and curious attendees.
- Creating the foundation for a recurring Houston meetup.
- Exploring where software freedom and digital rights meet practical community action.

## The short history

The free software movement began with the idea that people should have the freedom to run, study, share, and modify software. Open source later emphasized the practical benefits of collaborative development, broad adoption, and transparent engineering. Libre is often used as a bridge between those ideas.

Those traditions overlap a great deal. They share concerns about source access, user control, interoperability, and community participation, even if they emphasize different reasons for those goals.
