# LibreHTX 2026 Attendee Handout

Welcome to the inaugural LibreHTX 2026 meetup.

This gathering is for anyone curious about software freedom, open collaboration, privacy, digital rights, community-driven technology, or simply learning what these ideas mean. You do not need to be an expert, a developer, or already aligned with any particular tech organization to be here.

Our goal is to create a space where people can learn, ask questions, share ideas, and build a recurring community around technology that serves people well.

## What does "Libre” mean?

“Libre” means FREE in French. Not to refer to pricing or valuation. FREE as in a person having the liberty to run, study, share, and modify software. That idea is central to the free software concept and strongly overlaps with open source.

In practice, many people use the terms differently:

- **Free software** emphasizes user freedom and ethics.
- **Open source** emphasizes practical collaboration, transparency, and broad adoption.
- **Libre** often bridges both ideas and is used as a welcoming umbrella term.

## Where they overlap

Even when people prefer different words, the shared values are often the same:

- Access to source code.
- The ability to learn from and improve software.
- Community participation.
- Better control over the tools we use.

The main difference is emphasis, not total opposition. Many people care about both freedom and practical usefulness at the same time.

## Why we are gathering

We are gathering because we want to learn more and expand this community. We believe that communities grow best through regular conversations, not one-off events.

Our meetup can help us:

- Welcome people who are new to the topic.
- Explain the landscape without assuming prior knowledge.
- Share projects, tools, and ideas.
- Discover what kinds of future events would be valuable.

## FAQ

### Do I need a technical background?

Not at all. Beginners are welcome.

### Is this only for FSF supporters?

We do not discriminate on any basis. People of any affiliation and perspective are welcome; including persons who identify with open source, privacy, or are generally curious about tech.

### What if I do not know the terminology?

The point of the meetup is to share and keep the jargon-low and concepts understandable.

### Is there a “right” opinion here?

 No. The goal is respectful discussion and shared learning.

### Where can I find the glossary?

In the LibreHTX repository at docs/LEXICON.MD

### Why make this a regular event?

Because recurring meetings are more fun,build rapport, trust in shared progress, and the joy of seeeing awesome ideas turn into action.

### What can I expect to achieve?

A better understanding of the terms, new connections, and a clearer sense of why these ideas matter.
