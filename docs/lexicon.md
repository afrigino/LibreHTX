## Terms

**Free software**  
Software that protects the user's freedom to run, study, share, and modify it.

**Open source**  
Software development and licensing practices that emphasize source availability, collaboration, and practical benefits.

**Libre**  
A broad term often used to bridge free software and open source, with an emphasis on freedom.

**FSF**  
Free Software Foundation, a major advocate for software freedom.

**EFF**  
Electronic Frontier Foundation, a digital rights organization focused on privacy, speech, and user rights.

**Copyleft**  
A licensing approach that requires derivative works to preserve the same freedoms.

**Permissive license**  
A license that allows reuse and redistribution with relatively few conditions.
